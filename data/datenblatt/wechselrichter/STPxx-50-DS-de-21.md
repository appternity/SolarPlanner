# SMA Sunny Tripower X Datasheet
**STP 12-50 / STP 15-50 / STP 20-50 / STP 25-50**
*Innovative system solution for commercial and larger private PV systems.*

## Key Features
* **System Manager Function**: Monitoring and control for up to 5 inverters (max. 135 kVA) including direct access to Sunny Portal powered by ennexOS.
* **SMA Dynamic Power Control**: Integrated safety for dynamic regulation of active and reactive power.
* **High Safety Standards**: Includes SMA ArcFix (AFCI) for effective arc fault protection and DC overvoltage protection (optional).
* **Yield Optimization**: Integrated SMA ShadeFix for increased yield even during partial shading and I-V curve diagnostics.
* **Smart Connected**: SMA Smart Connected provides proactive error communication via Sunny Portal to operators and installers.
* **Scalability**: Modular expandability for future energy management functions.
