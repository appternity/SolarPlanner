# JA Solar Deep Blue 4.0 Datasheet
**JAM54D41 LB Series | n-type Bifacial Double Glass Module**
**Power Range: 430–455 W**

## Key Features
* **Technology**: n-type bifacial double glass module, Monocrystalline with high efficiency.
* **High Efficiency**: Improved low-light performance and better temperature coefficient.
* **Durability**: 25 years product warranty and 30 years linear power warranty.
* **Certifications**: IEC 61215, IEC 61730, ISO 9001, ISO 14001, ISO 45001, IEC 62941.
