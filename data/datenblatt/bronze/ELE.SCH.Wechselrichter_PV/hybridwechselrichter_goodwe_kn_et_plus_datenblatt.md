# GoodWe ET PLUS+ Series (16A) Hybrid Inverter Datasheet
**5 - 10kW | 2 MPPT | Three-Phase | HV EMEA**

The ET PLUS+ series hybrid inverters are the heart of an integrated PV and storage system. They are designed to maximize energy yield, optimize self-consumption, buffer peak loads, and provide backup power. With a wide battery voltage range, the system can be flexibly adapted to individual household needs. The ET PLUS+ series is compatible with a wide range of batteries, including the GoodWe Lynx Home F.

## Key Features
* **Smart Optimization**: Optimized energy autonomy and efficient operation.
* **Modern Design**: Compact and modern design.
* **High Safety Standards**: Highest safety standards integrated.
* **Silent Operation**: Fanless and quiet.
* **Smart Home Integration**: Ready for smart home ecosystems.
* **Backup Power**: Uninterruptible power supply (UPS) with switching time < 10ms.
