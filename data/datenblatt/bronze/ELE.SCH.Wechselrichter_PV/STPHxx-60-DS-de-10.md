# SMA Sunny Tripower Hybrid X Datasheet
**STPH5-60 / STPH6-60 / STPH8-60 / STPH10-60 / STPH12-60 / STPH15-60**
*3-phase Hybrid Inverter for flexible, reliable and future-proof energy supply in residential buildings.*

## Key Features
* **Flexible Power**: Range from 5 kVA to 15 kVA.
* **Advanced MPPT**: 3 independent MPP trackers with low PV input voltage (90 V).
* **Scalability**: Parallel operation of up to 5 hybrid inverters.
* **Integrated Energy Management**: Includes SMA Energy Maximizer (automatic optimization) and SMA Energy Planner.
* **Backup Solution**: Optional SMA Backup for full house power during outages.
* **Smart Integration**: Cloud-based management (ennexOS), SMA 360° App, and compatibility with SMA Home Storage / SMA Storage M (up to 37 kWh).
