# Vertex S TSM-NEG9RC.27 Datasheet
**Bifacial Double Glass N-type i-TOPCon Module**
**Power Range: 410–440 W**

## Key Features
* **High Efficiency**: Up to 440 W peak power with 22.0% module efficiency using high-density cell interconnection technology.
* **Temperature Performance**: Better temperature behavior at high ambient temperatures for increased power output.
* **Transparent Double Glass Concept**: Designed with aesthetics in mind, offering excellent resistance to fire and harsh environmental conditions.
* **Bifaciality**: Bifaciality factor up to 85%.
* **Durability**: 5,400 Pa snow load and 4,000 Pa wind load (test loads).

## Warranty
* **Product Warranty**: 25 years (covers manufacturing/workmanship).
* **Performance Warranty**: 30 years.
    * Year 1 max degradation: 1%.
    * Years 2–30 max annual degradation: 0.4%.
